Frequently asked questions

Can I keep using the computer after deleting files?

Avoid writing to the affected drive. Normal system activity can also create new writes when the deleted files were on the system drive.

Can every case be handled remotely?

No. The device must remain stable and suitable for logical work. Unusual noise, impact, liquid damage or complete non-detection usually needs hardware inspection.

Can recovery be guaranteed in advance?

No. Recoverability depends on media condition, overwriting and previous actions. Initial information only helps choose a path.
